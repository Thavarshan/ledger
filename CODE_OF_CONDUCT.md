# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Ledger a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect different experiences, backgrounds, and opinions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized language or attention, and other conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
